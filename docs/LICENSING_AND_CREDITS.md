## What credit is required?

Exhibit B specifies:

```text
Copyright (c) 2026 Cesare-ml (VirgoStars project)
Powered by VirgoStars
https://www.virgostars.it/
```

Exhibit B explicitly extends the attribution requirement to **Larger
Works** using covered code. CPAL Section 14 describes how attribution
must be prominently displayed when a GUI launches or a session begins.
A clickable link in an About/Credits screen is encouraged, but is not
automatically sufficient if it is never displayed at startup/session
initiation. The license does **not** require permanent branding on
every generated chart, or a particular logo. Programs with no GUI
are treated as described by Section 14(a); preserve source notices.

For example, an app with a graphical interface can show the three-line
attribution on its initial loading screen and also place a clickable
VirgoStars credit in the About section. This is guidance, not a legal
determination of compliance for every application.
